FixLink Service marketplace · 2026

A marketplace for the Philippines that connects customers with verified appliance repair technicians. It covers the full job: finding a technician, getting estimates, approving a quote, live chat, payment recording, and dispute handling.

  • Booking flow from estimate to quote to approval to completion, with realtime notifications and booking-based chat with read receipts.
  • Prepaid technician wallet that deducts a 10% commission automatically when a job is completed, and hides low-balance technicians from listings.
  • Technician verification through TESDA NC2, employment, business registration, or freelance references.
  • Admin portal with live bookings and a site name that updates across the UI, metadata, and emails.
  • Row Level Security on every table, responsive customer, technician, and admin portals, and Playwright end-to-end tests.
