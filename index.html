Security

I want systems secured like a fortress, not just checked off a list.

Most breaches don't start with something exotic. They start with a reused password, an unpatched server, or a policy nobody actually followed. I care more about closing that gap than passing an audit.

What I enjoy most is the human side of it: walking a team through what "secure by default" really means, and writing the policies that keep it that way after I've moved on.

  1. HardenBuild in depthReduce the attack surface at every layer, not just at the edge.
  2. TeachTrain the teamWalk coworkers through real threats, not just a slide deck.
  3. DocumentWrite the policyTurn good habits into rules that outlast me.
